## 2. Design Notes
* **Power:** The RC receiver passes the BEC voltage of the ESC (6.0 V or 7.4 V) to `VIN`. The 3.3 V regulator of the ESP32 board supplies the ESP32 and all peripherals.
* **Distribution:** The 3.3 V and GND lines of the peripherals are joined in two WAGO 221 lever connectors, shown as the two vertical rails on the right.
* **1-Wire:** Both DS18B20 probes are connected in parallel to `GPIO 4`. The required 4.7 kΩ pull-up resistor (R1) is located on the probe adapter board.
* **UART:** The lines are crossed: GPS TX to RX2 (`GPIO 16`), GPS RX to TX2 (`GPIO 17`).
* **Hall sensor:** The A3144 has an open-collector output. The internal pull-up of `GPIO 2` defines the high level.
* **Drawing conventions:** A dot marks a junction. Lines that cross without a dot are not connected.

## 3. Regenerating the Schematic
`Schematic_Graphical.svg` is generated by [`create_schematic.py`](create_schematic.py). The script uses only the Python standard library. Changes are made in the script, not in the SVG file:

```
python schematic/create_schematic.py
```
